The same in both options

  • The bar. It's surface with a hairline outline-variant top edge. The pale teal wash from tonal elevation goes, which lifts the unselected labels from 4.4:1 to 4.8:1. It's 64dp tall instead of 80, and it grows when the labels need room.
  • Labels. The selected label is on-surface SemiBold, not teal, because 12sp teal is 3.7:1. That also ends the "blue and teal at once" look. Unselected tabs are on-surface-variant.
  • Screen readers. Today the icon and the label both say "Home", so each tab is read twice. The icon goes silent, and the tab reads "Home, tab, 1 of 5, selected".
  • Cart. It's reached from the cart button on Browse. That button's spoken name changes from the hard-coded English "Cart" to "Open cart, 3 items", and its badge counts items rather than adding up quantities.

Large text (200%)

All the labels in a bar share one size. That's your text size if every label fits in two lines. Otherwise it's the largest size where they all fit, and never smaller than the normal 12sp. Nothing is cut off or ends in "…".
