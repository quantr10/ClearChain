Snackbar · Inventory · light. It confirms something you did. A refresh you started yourself no longer gets one.

Snackbars

  • Finding 4. The "… refreshed" snackbars go from all 13 places that send them. The pull spinner already shows when a refresh is done. This deletes one line in each of those ViewModels, plus the eight unused strings. Snackbars for failed refreshes stay.
  • One host. A shared ClearChainSnackbarHost replaces the 25 places that build their own SnackbarHost(…), including BrandScaffold and FabScaffold. It uses inverse-surface and inverse-on-surface on shape-extra-small, and its text wraps instead of being cut off. It sits above the bottom bar and over a FAB, as today.
  • Live-update snackbars, such as the admin's "new organization registered", stay as they are.

Empty states

  • Pull to refresh. Today the empty and error states on list screens sit outside the pull area, so you can't pull an empty list. They move inside it.
  • The count and sort row hides while the list is empty. Search and filter chips stay, so you can still change what you're looking at.
  • A next step, only where one is obvious. Nothing nearby on Browse gets "Change location", which opens the existing sheet. An empty Inventory or NGO Requests gets "Browse food". Filter-empty states get no button, because the chips are right above.
  • Large text. The empty state scrolls instead of clipping. Its spacing moves to tokens.

Loading

  • No change to the three kinds in LoadingIndicators.kt: spinner in the control, 40dp spinner in an empty area, 2dp bar on content being updated.
  • The area spinner appears after a short delay, so a fast load from the cache doesn't flash it. A screen reader hears "Loading".
  • On dashboards, the gradient header and greeting draw immediately, and the spinner sits in the sheet below.
  • Error states keep the warning icon with a retry button. Today it's labelled "Retry" on 13 screens and "Try again" on the two analytics screens. All of them say "Retry".
