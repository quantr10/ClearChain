The same in both options

  • Scrolling. The header scrolls away with the content. A teal strip stays pinned behind the status bar, as on Login, so the light status-bar icons stay readable over the cards.
  • Bell and avatar. They sit side by side at the end of the greeting row. The bell is a 36dp disc and keeps its unread badge. The avatar is 40dp with a white ring.
  • Screen readers. The greeting is a heading. The bell says "Open notifications, 5 unread" as one phrase; today the badge is read separately. The avatar opens Account details, so it says "Open account details" instead of "Profile picture of …" (or just the initial).
  • Profile. Same gradient, same status-bar treatment. The 84dp avatar tile becomes 64dp, and the hero loses its fixed 248dp height, so it grows with large text instead of clipping.
  • Every other screen (lists, details, settings) keeps a plain background and status bar. That's the next question.
