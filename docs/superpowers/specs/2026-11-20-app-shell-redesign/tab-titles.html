Tab screens: show a title or not?

Today Listings, Requests, Browse, Inventory, Verify and History open straight onto a search row with no title, while Stats has a large one. Each option shows grocery Listings (light, en), admin History (dark, en) and admin Stats (light, vi). Cropped, at 62%.

A

The search row is the header (my pick)

List tabs keep today's layout. The selected tab already says where you are, and the search hint names the list. A screen reader announces the screen's name when the tab opens, through a pane title nobody sees. Stats has no search row, so it gets the standard title row in place of its oversized title. This keeps the most room for cards on a 640dp-tall screen.
